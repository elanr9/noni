# FieldVision voice doc v1

Paste into brand_docs.voice. loadBrandContext ships this into ingest-brief,
generate-script and generateTaskDraft. Replace the whole prose voice
description with this. Descriptions of tone do not produce speech that
sounds real. Exemplars do.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS DOC (instruction to the model)

Match the cadence of the transcripts below. Do not imitate a brand tone
description. These are real spoken words from the two creators who will
perform the script. Write something one of these two people could say out
loud without changing a word.

Before returning the draft, check it against RULES. If it fails any rule,
rewrite.

## FORMAT LIBRARY (only these four; they have performed nothing else)

1. Numbered advice list. "Here's 10 things I wish I knew." Credential
   opener, then numbered items, product appears as one item late.
2. Personal anecdote into product. Real conversation or memory, the
   mistake it exposes, then how the product fixed it for them.
3. Question answer explainer. Opens with a question people keep asking
   them, answers from both sides of their experience.
4. Training voiceover. Narrating b roll of a solo session. No product.

If a source post uses a format not on this list (two hander skits, POV
sketches, green screen reacts), do not copy its structure. Map it to the
nearest format above. Never write dialogue between two speakers.

---

## RULES (hard checks before returning JSON)

1. Single speaker, first person. No second voice, no interviewer, no
   "Wait what?"
2. At least two real proper nouns from the creator's fact sheet in the
   first half. School names, club names, positions.
3. Product first mention must land between 55 and 85 percent through the
   script. Measured in the real corpus: 53, 57, 68, 83, 86 percent, and one
   video with no mention at all.
4. Product described by mechanism, not benefit. "It wrote the emails and
   sent them and followed up." Not "it builds a portfolio that proves your
   soccer IQ."
5. Value before pitch. The viewer must get something usable even if they
   never touch the product.
6. No three item parallel lists inside a sentence.
7. No sentence that could appear on a landing page.
8. Allow restarts, "like," "you know," "trust me," "not gonna lie," "mind
   you," "guys," "brother." These are in the corpus. They are correct.
9. CTA matches the real pattern: comment a single word, creator sends a
   free trial. Not "click the link."
